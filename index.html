Our App

Openframe A video editor, photo editor and file converter built for screen reader users.

Openframe can be used entirely from the keyboard and works with screen readers such as NVDA. Edit video on a timeline, retouch photos, and convert files, all with spoken feedback for every action.

  • Timeline video editing with filters, transitions, text and captions
  • Photo editing with crop, resize, filters and spoken photo descriptions
  • Conversion between video, audio, image and document formats
  • A built-in assistant that takes plain-English commands
  • Every keyboard shortcut can be changed

Version 3.6.0 · Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit · 96 MB. Video and audio features need FFmpeg.

Lighthouse for OBS Studio

Lighthouse A plugin that makes OBS Studio usable and fun with the NVDA screen reader.

Lighthouse gives OBS a voice. It labels OBS's own windows for NVDA and adds an all-in-one Command Center built for the keyboard, so you can stream and record without needing to see the screen.

  • Global keys that control OBS from anywhere, even inside a game
  • Add, arrange and adjust scenes, sources and audio using plain words
  • Spoken warnings if you talk while muted, your mic clips, or your picture goes black
  • Hear what your stream looks like, and have text on the output read aloud
  • Pre-flight and mic checks, original sound cues, and updates that always ask first

Version 1.3.1 · Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit · OBS Studio 32 or newer · 3 MB. Windows SmartScreen may show a warning because the installer isn't code-signed.

Touchline for iPhone and iPad

Touchline An offline football management game, fully playable with VoiceOver.

Found a club and run it from the boardroom, or take a manager's job and win matches from the dugout. Every screen works with VoiceOver, and full club names are always read aloud instead of abbreviations.

  • Owner and manager careers with staff, facilities, sponsors, tactics, training and a kit designer
  • A minute-by-minute match engine with detailed spoken commentary, a reactive crowd and haptics
  • A full football pyramid with promotion, relegation, cups and international tournaments
  • Real 2026-27 squads across 34 leagues, with transfers, renewals, a youth academy and AI clubs
  • Works completely offline, with no accounts and no adverts

Version 2.3.0 · iOS and iPadOS 17 or newer · 7 MB · Unsigned IPA, install it with a sideloading tool such as Feather.
